Two boards, one cost axis. Every number below is either measured in this repository's own artifacts or read from the paper cited beside it — no value is interpolated.

1. RCABench — Acc@1 of the ten published configurations, and this project's four surfaces

The ten grey bars are `arXiv:2608.21310` Table 2, measured by those authors on a 500-case random sample of the 1 430 released cases. The four red bars are this project on all 1 422 cases: the deterministic engine alone (1 156 correct), the seeded LLM arm (1 274), and the arm that shows no ranked order and settles every refusal on the engine (1 286.3 over three runs = 90.46 %). The populations differ, so the placement is directional — and the one sentence that has to travel with the top bar is that of the 130 cases between the engine and it, 100 are two deterministic resolvers and 30 are the model.

2. RCAEval — the cells a published reference exists on, ours against it

Paired by board cell, because a suite average is not comparable to a single-cell number. RE1 92.0 % (345/375), RE2 92.2 % (249/270), RE3 80.0 % (72/90) — 666/735 = 90.61 % overall. The RE3 pair is the one that is not the same population: PRISM's 76.7 % is its own conjunctive magnitude read as a ranker on TrainTicket alone, and on that cell a published number is still above ours — the one place in this document where that is true.

3. Accuracy against cost — the axis that separates a product from a demonstration

Cost per case in CNY, logarithmic, on an axis that spans four orders of magnitude. The solid line joins the two points with the same accuracy: 90.46 % here (three runs: 1 285 / 1 287 / 1 287) against 90.00 % there, at ¥0.0146 against ¥32.94 — 2 253×. The dashed line is the engine's own 81.29 % at ¥0: the distance between it and the top of the chart is the model's contribution, and it is 29 cases. The engine and the two arms are one product population measured on 1 422 cases; the Sonnet row is a leaderboard population on 500, and the 0.46 pp between them is measured on this side by three runs whose spread is 0.14 pp, which is stated rather than drawn. Points with no published token count are absent rather than drawn at zero.
